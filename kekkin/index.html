A first guide based on the Labour Standards Act (Art. 24, 39, 91 and 115), pages of the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare and Labour Bureaus, and Kyokai Kenpo's sickness allowance page (checked November 2026). It does not calculate yen: how a company counts one day's pay is in its own wage rules. Company sick leave schemes and health insurance society extras are not included. Not legal advice. Always confirm with your company's HR, your insurer or the Labour Standards Inspection Office.
