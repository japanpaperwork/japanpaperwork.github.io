Hurt at work, then left the job: rosai claim planner

For people in Japan who were hurt or got sick because of work or on their commute, and have left the job (or will soon). Enter your dates. You get whether the next leave claim needs your former employer's certificate, the 2-year limit for your oldest unclaimed days, and the Hello Work dates if you cannot look for work yet. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere; it stays in this page.

A first guide based on the Industrial Accident Compensation Insurance Act (Art. 12-5, Art. 42), pages of the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare, Labour Bureaus and Hello Work (checked November 2026). Whether a case is work-related, and how each claim is handled, is decided by the Labour Standards Inspection Office. Hello Work decides the unemployment benefit. Not legal advice. Always confirm with the office.
