Furusato nozei: planner

For people living and paying tax in Japan who want to try furusato nozei (ふるさと納税, a donation to a city or prefecture that lowers your income tax and residence tax). It shows whether the one-stop exception can work for you or you need a tax return, your deadlines, and the traps for foreign residents. It does not calculate your limit. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere; it stays in this page.

A first guide based on pages of the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (furusato nozei portal), the National Tax Agency and local governments (checked November 2026).
