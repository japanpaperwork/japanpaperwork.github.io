Pregnant in Japan: boshi techo and check-up planner

For foreign residents in Japan who are pregnant (or whose partner is). Enter your due date and a few facts. You get your check-up rhythm by date, when you can apply for the pregnancy support payment, what to bring to the city office, and what to do if you give birth in another city or move. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere; it stays in this page.
