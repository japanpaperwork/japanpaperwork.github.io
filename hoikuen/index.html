Nursery school (hoikuen) in Japan: planner

For foreign parents in Japan who want a place at a licensed nursery school (認可保育園). You apply to your city, not to the nursery, and the city needs a reason why you cannot care for the child in the daytime. Enter your child's birth date, the month you want to start and your situation. You get the class age, the dates to watch, the order of the steps and the papers. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere; it stays in this page.
