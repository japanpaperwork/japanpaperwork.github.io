A first guide based on the Labour Standards Act (Art. 39 and 115) and pages of the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare and Labour Bureaus (checked November 2026). It shows the minimum the law gives; your company may give more or give it earlier. Public holidays and company days off are not counted in the dates. Not legal advice. Always confirm with your company's HR or the Labour Standards Inspection Office.
