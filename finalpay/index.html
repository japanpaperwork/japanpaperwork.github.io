Final pay and unpaid wages: planner

For people who left (or will leave) a job at a company in Japan and are waiting for their last pay, or were not paid in full. Enter your last day and your company's pay rule. You get the date your pay is due, the 7-day date if you ask in writing, the time limit to claim, and where to go. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere; it stays in this page.
