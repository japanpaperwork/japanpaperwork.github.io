A first guide based on the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare's standard on fixed-term contracts and non-renewal (amended April 2024), its page on leaving, dismissal and non-renewal, the Labor Contract Act (Art. 17, 18 and 19), Hello Work's page on company-reason and special-reason leavers, and Immigration Services Agency pages (checked November 2026). Total years are counted from the first start day to the current end day; gaps between contracts can change the 5-year count. It does not judge whether a non-renewal is valid or which Hello Work group you are in. Not legal advice. Always confirm with the Labour Standards Inspection Office or a General Labour Consultation Corner.
