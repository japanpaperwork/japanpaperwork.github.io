This is a guide based on pages of Yokohama, Nagoya, Sendai, Saitama and Kagoshima cities. Each city decides its own forms, deadlines, due dates and when deduction starts. The bill months used here (end of June, August, October and January) are common, but your tax notice shows your own dates. This page does not calculate any tax amount. Confirm with HR and the tax section of your city or ward office.
