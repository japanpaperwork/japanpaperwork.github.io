A rough first check based on public pages of the Japan Pension Service (employees' pension 18.3%, split in half), the Japan Health Insurance Association (FY2026 prefecture rates from the March 2026 insurance month, nursing care 1.62%, child support levy 0.23% from April 2026), the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (employment insurance from April 2026: worker share 5/1,000, or 6/1,000 for farming, forestry, fishing, sake brewing and construction), the National Tax Agency (withholding tables) and the Tokyo Metropolitan Government (resident tax from June to May), checked November 2026. Health insurance and pension use a standard monthly amount (標準報酬月額), not this month's pay, so they can differ from this check. This page does not calculate income tax or resident tax. Not tax or legal advice. Always confirm with your company's HR.
