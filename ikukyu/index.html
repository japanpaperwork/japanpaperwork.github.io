Childcare leave benefits in Japan: date & money planner

For foreign residents in Japan who plan childcare leave (育児休業, ikuji kyugyo) from a job with employment insurance. Enter the birth date, your leave dates and your pay. You get the benefit periods, a rough amount at 67% and 50%, the extra 13% when both parents take leave, the first claim deadline and the daycare extension dates. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere; it stays in this page.
