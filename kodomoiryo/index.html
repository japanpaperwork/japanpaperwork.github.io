Child medical card in Japan: planner

For foreign parents in Japan with a baby or a child. Most cities pay part or all of a child's share of medical bills, but only after you apply for the city's medical card (医療証). Enter your child's birth date and your situation. You get the dates that matter, the order of the steps, the papers, and how to get money back when you paid at the counter. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere; it stays in this page.
