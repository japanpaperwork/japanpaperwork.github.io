Found a job early? Re-employment allowance checker

For people in Japan who were approved for the basic allowance (unemployment benefit) at Hello Work and found a new job. Enter your dates and numbers. You get the days left, the rate (60% or 70%), a rough amount, the deadline to apply, and the conditions to check. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere; it stays in this page.
