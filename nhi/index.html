Watch out

Dates are a guide based on general rules published by the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare, Japan Health Insurance Association (Kyokai Kenpo) and city offices. Each city runs its own National Health Insurance, so documents, premiums and payment schedules differ. Holidays are not counted here. Always confirm with your city office (and your insurer for continuing a company plan).
