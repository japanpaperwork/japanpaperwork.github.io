Dismissal notice in Japan: checker

For employees in Japan who were told they will be dismissed (解雇, kaiko). Enter the day you were told and your last day. You see how many days of notice you got, whether 30 days' notice or notice pay (解雇予告手当, kaiko yokoku teate) applies, a rough amount, the papers to ask for and the next deadlines. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere; it stays in this page.

A first guide based on the Labour Standards Act (Art. 12, 19, 20, 21, 22 and 23), the Labor Contract Act (Art. 16 and 17), the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare's page on leaving, dismissal and non-renewal, Hello Work's page on company-reason leavers, and Immigration Services Agency pages (checked November 2026). The amount is a rough estimate with the basic method only; the company's figure can differ (for example, the exact 3 months and items of pay). This page counts notice days from the day after you were told; if your count is close, ask the office. It does not judge whether a dismissal is valid. Not legal advice. Always confirm with the Labour Standards Inspection Office or a General Labour Consultation Corner.
