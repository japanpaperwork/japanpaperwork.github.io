Injured at work or on your commute: rosai claim planner

For employees in Japan who were hurt or got sick because of work, or were hurt on the way to or from work. Enter your dates and your situation. You get the forms to use, the waiting days, the day the leave benefit starts, your 2-year deadlines and your next steps. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere; it stays in this page.
