Watch out

A guide based on pages of the Japan Health Insurance Association (Kyokai Kenpo) on voluntary continuation and city pages on National Health Insurance (checked November 2026). Weekends and national holidays (2026 to 2028) move the deadlines; the year-end closure is not counted, so confirm dates near New Year. Health insurance unions have their own forms and premium rules. The premium is a rough estimate; the payment slip from your insurer decides. Please confirm with your insurer and your city office.
