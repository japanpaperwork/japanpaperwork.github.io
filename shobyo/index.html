Sick leave pay in Japan (shobyo teate): date planner

For employees with company health insurance (shakai hoken) who cannot work because of an illness or injury that is not work-related. Enter your dates. You get the 3 waiting days, the first day that can be paid, the longest period, the claim deadlines, a rough daily amount, and what happens if you quit. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere; it stays in this page.
