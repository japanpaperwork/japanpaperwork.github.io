Pension record check: planner

For people working or living in Japan who want to see their Japanese pension record online on Nenkin Net (ねんきんネット, the Japan Pension Service's website). It shows which way to register fits you, how long it takes, when your next Nenkin Teikibin (ねんきん定期便, the yearly pension statement) should come, and what to check. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere; it stays in this page.
