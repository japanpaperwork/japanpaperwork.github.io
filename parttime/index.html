Part-time work limit in Japan: hours checker

For students (留学) and family members (家族滞在) in Japan with permission to work part-time (資格外活動許可, shikakugai katsudo kyoka). Enter your shifts for 2 weeks, all jobs together. It checks them against the 28-hour rule and the 8-hour rule in school vacations, and shows what to do. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere; it stays in this page.
