Just married in Japan: notices & deadline planner

For foreign residents who just married in Japan (or married abroad and live here). Enter the marriage date and what changes for you: name, address, health insurance. You get each deadline in date order (residence card, moving or household notice, dependent insurance, leaving kokuho, the Japanese spouse's family name), what to bring, and what to tell later. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere; it stays in this page.
