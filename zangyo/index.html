A rough first check based on the Labour Standards Act (Art. 32, 35, 36, 37, 41 and 115) and the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare's page on overtime and wages (checked November 2026). It uses the legal minimum rates only: 25% for overtime, 50% for overtime over 60 hours a month, 25% extra for 22:00 to 5:00, 35% for the legal day off. Your company may pay more. Yen are rounded. Not legal advice and not a wage calculation for your case. Always confirm with your company's HR or the Labour Standards Inspection Office.
