This is a guide based on the Immigration Control Act and pages of the Immigration Services Agency of Japan. It counts 14 days from the day of the divorce or death and shows a safer target a few days earlier. It does not judge whether you can stay, which status fits you, or whether you have a justifiable reason, and it does not fill in any form. Always confirm with the regional immigration office or the Immigration Services Information Center (0570-013904).
