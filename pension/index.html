Watch out

Dates are a guide based on general rules published by the Japan Pension Service and city offices. Premium amounts change every April. Holidays are not counted for the 14-day deadline; a due date on a weekend or holiday moves to the next business day (only weekends are moved here). Whether an exemption is approved is decided by the Japan Pension Service after you apply. Always confirm with your city office or a pension office.
