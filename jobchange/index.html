Watch out

This is a guide based on pages of the Immigration Services Agency of Japan. It counts 14 days from the day of the change (your last day, or the date of the new contract) and shows a safer target a few days earlier. A notice cannot be sent before the change happens. This page does not judge whether your new job fits your status, and it does not fill in any form. Always confirm with the Immigration Services Agency (Immigration Services Information Center: 0570-013904).
