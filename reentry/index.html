This is a guide based on pages of the Immigration Services Agency of Japan (re-entry permit, special re-entry permit, and its Q&A, checked November 2026). The "last safe day" is set one day before the one-year (two-year, or two-month) mark, to be safe. This page does not decide your case. People in some situations (for example, a procedure to revoke their status, or refugee applicants with "Designated Activities") cannot use the special re-entry permit. Confirm with the regional immigration office or the Immigration Services Information Center (0570-013904).
