Watch out

This is a guide based on the Japan Pension Service's claim form instructions for the Lump-sum Withdrawal Payment (September 2026 edition). It does not calculate the amount or any tax, and it does not decide whether you qualify. The Japan Pension Service decides that when it receives your claim. Always confirm with the Japan Pension Service or a pension office (年金事務所).
